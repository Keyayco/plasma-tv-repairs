What we fix

Every common — and uncommon — TV failure.

From dead power boards to ghostly lines on the panel, we have the tools, the parts, and the patience to get it right.

Power & Standby Faults

Dead TV, blinking standby light, won't turn on? Power board and capacitor repairs done in-house.

No Picture / Black Screen

Backlight rebuilds, T-CON board swaps, and panel diagnostics for the dreaded "sound but no picture" issue.

Lines, Flicker & Ghosting

Vertical or horizontal lines, dim halves, flickering backlights — we trace it to the source.

HDMI & Input Boards

Burnt HDMI ports, dead inputs, no signal errors. Mainboard repair or replacement when needed.

Smart TV Software

Boot loops, frozen logos, broken WebOS/Tizen/Android TV firmware — flashed and restored.

Free Diagnostics

Bring it in — we'll tell you what's wrong, what it'll cost, and whether it's worth saving. No surprises.

FAQ

The questions we hear every day.

How much does a TV repair cost?

Most repairs land between a small service fee and roughly half the price of a new TV. We give a firm quote after diagnosis — and you decide.

How long does it take?

Diagnosis is usually within 24 hours. Most repairs are finished in 2–5 working days, depending on parts.

Do you repair Plasma TVs?

Yes — it's literally in our name. Plasma, LED, LCD, OLED, and Smart TVs from any major brand.

What if my TV can't be fixed?

Then you pay nothing for the diagnosis. We'll tell you honestly when a repair isn't economical.

Do you offer a warranty?

Every repair comes with a 90-day warranty on the work and replacement parts.
